Purpose

A living archive, not merely a plant list.

This site documents individual plants over time: how they were received, how their identification changes, how they grow, when they flower, and what happens after controlled pollination.

Labels are treated as hypotheses when provenance is uncertain. Original nursery names are preserved alongside current or tentative identifications, so that useful information is not lost.

Areas of work

01

Living collection

Accession-based records for species, natural hybrids, cultivated hybrids, unidentified material and seed-grown plants.

02

Pollination & seed

Controlled crosses, pollen storage, fruit development, seed harvest, germination and long-term seedling variation.

03

Taxonomy & sourcing

Synonyms, historic nursery records, field numbers, locality data, questionable labels and searches for genetically distinct clones.

About the project

Independent cultivation and documentation in Ottawa.

Ottawa Epiphytic Cactaceae is an amateur living-collection project focused on preserving records, sharing observations and improving the traceability of cultivated epiphytic cacti.

The project does not claim formal taxonomic authority. Identifications are presented with confidence levels, references and corrections as additional evidence becomes available.
